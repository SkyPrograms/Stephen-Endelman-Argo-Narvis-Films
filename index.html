02 / About

A life shaped
by music and film.

Stephen Endelman is a composer and producer whose work spans film, television, theatre and concert music.

Born in London, Endelman studied music at the Purcell School and Guildhall School of Music and Drama before building a career composing for the screen. His work moves between orchestral storytelling, contemporary musical language and character-led scores—always in service of the world on screen.

From independent cinema to larger-scale productions, his projects reflect a deep interest in the relationship between sound, image and human experience.
